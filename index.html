I / Little discoveries

Room for what is.

☾ · ✦ · ❧

Lately I've been noticing how much of life I once treated as something to manage: thoughts, feelings, other people's experiences, the future, even the things I enjoyed.

And how different it feels to simply notice something without needing to make anything of it.

Things I keep noticingA feeling doesn't have to become a task.
A familiar story doesn't have to be a permanent truth.
A beautiful moment doesn't have to lead anywhere.
Enjoyment doesn't need a reason.
Space doesn't need to be filled.

I'm still curious about perception, consciousness, and what it's like to be alive. I just don't need that curiosity to become a method, a mission, or an answer.

No instructions. Just observations along the way.
