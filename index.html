Coming back into relationship with yourself

The moment you leave yourself is often invisible.

01 / The work

What happens before the story?

Something happens. Your body responds. Responsibility is silently assumed. Pressure develops. Then you manage, fix, explain, comply, withdraw, or disappear.

By the time the worry or story arrives, it can feel like reality itself. My work creates space around this sequence so you can see what became authority—and where choice still lives.

“You can be affected without turning the experience into an assignment.”

The alternative is not passivity. It is staying in relationship with yourself before deciding what the moment means or requires.

02 / About Megan

A quiet place to notice what is already happening.

I notice what does not quite reconcile—the place where a feeling, story, responsibility, and identity have become compressed into one experience.

My work is to create enough space for those pieces to become visible again. Through careful listening, thoughtful questions, and language for the underlying mechanism, I help people hear their own recognition without replacing it with mine.

I am interested in what becomes possible when understanding returns you to your own authority.

03 / Work with me

A place to hear what is actually happening.

Coaching with me is a space to slow down what feels compressed, separate what has become merged, and find the mechanism underneath the pressure. I do not become the authority over your experience. We create the conditions for your own recognition to become available again.
