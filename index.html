I / Staying Here

I kept thinking I needed to understand myself before I could move forward.

☾ · ✦ · ❧

For a long time, understanding helped me come back to myself. I followed patterns, found old rules, traced reactions to their origins, and made sense of things that had once felt invisible. Lately I have been noticing the moment that same movement becomes a way of leaving the present.

One expression of thisLife Hushing grew from Myquillyn Smith’s idea of House Hushing—quieting a home so it can breathe. It still matters to me. I still notice what I am maintaining simply because it is already here. But now I see that as one expression of something deeper: staying with what is here instead of leaving the moment to manage it.

I can feel something without tracing it backward. I can want something without making its absence mean I lack something. I can dislike a condition without turning it into an authority. I can let a moment be difficult without making anything wrong. The movement is becoming simpler: stay in contact with what is here, and move from here.

Notice what is here Let the feeling be real Stop turning it into a problem Leave the past where it is Move from this moment

I do not need to understand the origin of this moment in order to live from it.

✦ A recognition
“I can stay here without turning myself into something to solve.”

Peace does not always come from finding the right explanation. Sometimes it comes from letting a feeling be a feeling, a preference be a preference, and a moment be a moment—without turning any of them into evidence that something is wrong.

III / About

I’m Megan. I notice patterns easily, and for a long time I followed every thread backward until I could explain what was happening. That gave me a lot. Now I’m interested in something quieter: what happens when I no longer need an explanation before I trust what is here.

For a long time, I assumed every meaningful discovery needed to become something: content, work, an offer, a practice, a better way to live. I also assumed every difficult feeling needed to be understood. I am less interested in doing either now. I want this site to be a place where I can write from what is alive without requiring the writing—or myself—to become anything else.

Contact before explanation. Presence before interpretation. Move from here.

IV / Write back

The page stays quiet. You can still write back.

I do not want a public comment section here. I like that this space stays mine—without reactions, debates, or other voices collecting underneath the writing.

But I do love the possibility of correspondence. If something I write stirs something in you, you can send me a private note. No public performance. Just one person writing back to another.

✦

You do not need to have a polished response. A thought, a recognition, a question, or simply “this stayed with me” is enough.
