I / Start Here

I thought the work was changing myself. Now I see the starting point was the whole thing.

☾ · ✦ · ❧

For a long time, I started with what was happening outside me. A mood. A number. A responsibility. A craving. A reaction. A goal. Then I asked what it meant, what it required from me, and what I needed to change so I could get somewhere else.

The shiftNot better management. Not a better strategy. Not a better version of me. A different place to begin.

I can begin with myself first. What do I know? What do I want? What am I choosing? Then I can meet whatever is here without making it the authority over me. The outside can matter. It can affect me. It can give me information. It does not have to tell me who I am or what is possible.

Start with myself Know what I want Let the outside be information Choose from here Live instead of manage

Self first. Circumstance second.

✦ A recognition
“I do not have to use my life to figure out who I am.”

Something can happen without becoming a verdict. A feeling can be here without becoming a problem. Someone can react without becoming my authority. A desire can exist without turning me into a project. There is so much less to manage when I stop asking the outside to tell me where to begin.

III / About

I’m Megan. I notice patterns easily. For a long time, that meant following every thread until I understood what was happening, then trying to use that understanding to change myself or my life.

Now I am interested in what comes before the strategy, the system, the role, the explanation, and the improvement project. I keep finding the same quiet place: I am already here. I can know what I want before the world tells me what anything means. I can let other people be themselves without making their experience my instruction. I can let my own life be lived instead of constantly managed.

Start with myself. Let the rest come second.

IV / Write back

The page stays quiet. You can still write back.

I do not want a public comment section here. I like that this space stays mine—without reactions, debates, or other voices collecting underneath the writing.

But I do love correspondence. If something I write opens something in you, you can send me a private note. No performance. No need to agree. Just one person writing back to another.

✦

You do not need to have a polished response. A thought, a recognition, a question, or simply “this stayed with me” is enough.
