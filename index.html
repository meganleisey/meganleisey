I / Little discoveries

Letting it be enough.

☾ · ✦ · ❧

Lately I've been noticing how much of life I once treated as something to manage: my time, my feelings, other people's experiences, even the things I loved.

These days I love the quiet: the windows open, the cats nearby, the warmth of morning light. And how different it feels to enjoy a moment without needing to make anything of it.

Things I keep noticingRest doesn't have to be earned.
A familiar story doesn't have to be a permanent truth.
Comfort doesn't need an excuse.
Enjoyment doesn't need a reason.
Space doesn't need to be filled.

I'm still curious about perception, consciousness, and what it's like to be alive. I love the little realizations that rise up when there's room for them. And I love the mornings when nothing in particular happens.

A small collection of things I notice and love.
