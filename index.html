I / Reflections

When feeling becomes doing.

☾ · ✦ · ❧

I've been noticing how quickly an ordinary feeling can become something I believe I have to do. Anger becomes a demand to change something. Curiosity becomes a demand to understand. Even excitement can become a responsibility to preserve or produce something.

It isn't about becoming calm, unaffected, or less caring. It's about discovering the difference between experiencing a feeling and treating it as an instruction.

The automatic patternI feel something → it becomes an assignment → authority, responsibility and urgency → my own wanting becomes secondary.

And another possibility: I feel something. I can experience it. I remain free to choose. Feelings can still inform my choices, and real needs and responsibilities still matter. I don't have to make every feeling into a job.

Even this discovery doesn't have to become an assignment.

II / Field notes

Little reflections.

Ideas and observations that have taken on a life of their own here. No lesson to deliver, no destination to reach.

01 · Perception

The experience doesn't tell you anything.

I've been fascinated by the meanings we give what happens around us. What if an experience could simply be an experience, without becoming a conclusion about what's possible?

An observation doesn't have to become a verdict.

02 · Allowing

Not here to there. Just there.

I love this image of putting on the experience you desire, like putting on clothes. Not figuring out how to travel toward a feeling, but enjoying the feeling itself.

No journey required.

03 · Awareness

A different kind of curiosity.

I can follow a question because it delights me, not because there's a problem waiting to be solved. Some of my favorite discoveries begin with a simple 'I wonder...'

Curiosity can be its own reward.

04 · Possibility

What else is possible?

I love imagining possibilities without needing to turn them into plans. Sometimes it's enough to enjoy the thought, follow it somewhere unexpected, and see what else appears.

There's always more to imagine.

05 · Connection

The joy of connection.

A conversation that goes somewhere surprising. Laughing until it hurts. Feeling completely at ease with someone. I love connection that doesn't need an agenda.

Just being together.

06 · Wonder

The magic of little lights.

A room can feel sad in the dimness, then completely different with a little brightness. A glowing tree, a tiny illuminated village, warm lights against the dark — the same room, a new experience.

Light changes the feeling of everything.

A collection that can change whenever something new catches my attention. ✦
