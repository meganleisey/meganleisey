Overview

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit meganleisey.vercel.app or choose to connect with Megan Leisey through a service linked from the website.

Information you provide

This website does not currently include a contact form, account system, newsletter signup, or online payment. If you schedule a free conversation through Calendly, you may provide information such as your name, email address, scheduling preferences, and anything you choose to include with your booking. Megan receives the information needed to respond to and manage that booking.

Information collected automatically

The services that host and display this website may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and the date and time of your visit. How information is used

Information is used only to respond to you, manage scheduled conversations, provide the website, maintain its security and performance, and comply with legal obligations. Megan Leisey does not sell your personal information.

Other services and websites

The website links to Calendly, YouTube, PangoBooks, and potentially other independent services. When you follow those links, their own privacy policies and practices apply. Calendly processes booking information on its platform.

Retention and your choices

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was provided, recordkeeping, or legal obligations. You may ask what personal information Megan holds about you or request a correction or deletion, subject to any information that must be retained for legal or legitimate business reasons.

Children’s privacy

This website and its coaching services are not directed to children under 13, and Megan does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website and its services change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
